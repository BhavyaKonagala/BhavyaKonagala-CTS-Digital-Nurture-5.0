# Fundamentals of Unit Testing

## TestFixture

Represents a test class.

---

## SetUp

Runs before every test.

---

## TearDown

Runs after every test.

---

## TestCase

Allows one method to execute multiple test cases.

Example

[TestCase(5,true)]

[TestCase(8,false)]

---

## Ignore

Skips a test.

---

## Good Unit Test

✔ Small

✔ Fast

✔ Independent

✔ Repeatable

✔ Readable
